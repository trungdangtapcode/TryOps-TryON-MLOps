# Presentation Outline (15 slides) + Demo Scripts

## Slide sequence

1. **Title** — TryOps: Enterprise MLOps for Governed VTON and Efficient LLM Serving.
2. **The problem** — ML demos are easy; production ML is hard (the 90% around the model).
3. **The thesis** — MLOps is the product; models are governed assets. The claim: no model ships without evidence, a gate, and lineage.
4. **Workloads** — VTON (vision, GPU-heavy) + LLM optimization (latency/memory-bound) as opposite stress tests.
5. **Architecture** — the ten-layer diagram; simulation-first spine with real backends behind unchanged contracts.
6. **Data & artifact lifecycle** — versioning, data/model cards, run context.
7. **Pipeline & registry** — DAG, candidate→champion, policy gates, signed PR promotion trigger.
8. **The money shot** — `make validate-bad`: the policy gate *blocks* a bad model.
9. **LLM optimization result** — the quantization Pareto table; 4-bit recommended, 8-bit dominated.
10. **Native boundary** — Go signed-PR/registry/guardrail services plus Go load driver, Rust gateway artifact, and seventeen compiled C++ engines on hot paths; "Python is the lab, not the product."
11. **Green MLOps** — the energy table; quantization *costs* energy here (the surprising finding).
12. **Observability & governance** — dashboards, NIST/OWASP mapping, carbon-aware gate.
13. **Reliability** — one-command rollback + incident drill.
14. **Honesty** — the Reality Ledger and self-audit; what's real vs simulated.
15. **Evaluation summary + future work** — maturity numbers; Wave 2 themes M–U.
